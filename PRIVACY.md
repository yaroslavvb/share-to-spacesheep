# Privacy Policy — spacesheep (Unofficial Share Client)

**Effective Date:** October 21, 2026

**spacesheep** (`dev.spacesheep.share`) is an independent, open-source, unofficial third-party community application created by Yaroslav Bulatov ([@yaroslavvb](https://github.com/yaroslavvb/share-to-spacesheep)). It is not built by, affiliated with, or endorsed by the Spacesheep team (`spacesheep.dev`).

## Data Collection and Use

1. **No Third-Party Analytics or Tracking:** This application does not include any analytics SDKs, advertising frameworks, crash trackers, or telemetry servers operated by the developer.
2. **Authentication Credentials:** When you sign in to `spacesheep.dev` or enter a `spacesheep.dev` API key (`ss_…`), the token is stored locally on your device (`SharedPreferences` on Android / `UserDefaults` on iOS) and is transmitted exclusively over HTTPS (`https://mcp.spacesheep.dev/mcp` and `https://spacesheep.dev`) to authenticate your requests.
3. **Shared Content:** Webpages, URLs, images, PDFs, and files that you explicitly choose to share to the app via the system Share sheet or file picker are processed locally on your device and uploaded directly to your account on `https://spacesheep.dev`. The app does not transmit your content to any other server.

## Data Retention and Deletion

- You can remove your stored API key at any time by tapping **Disconnect** inside the app or uninstalling the application.
- Spaces published to your `spacesheep.dev` account are governed by your settings on `https://spacesheep.dev`.

## Contact

For questions or issues regarding this application, please open an issue at [https://github.com/yaroslavvb/share-to-spacesheep/issues](https://github.com/yaroslavvb/share-to-spacesheep/issues).
